# CMS Operations Runbook

1. Confirm tenant, content identifier, locale, revision and workflow state.
2. Verify author, reviewer, approver and publication audit records.
3. Quarantine suspected malicious, infringing or sensitive content.
4. Validate sanitisation, malware scan, source attribution and accessibility metadata.
5. For bilingual content, compare English and Tamil publication status and semantic parity.
6. Use audited rollback to a known revision; never edit production records directly.
7. Purge caches only after the authoritative revision is confirmed.
8. Record customer impact, mitigation, recovery evidence and follow-up actions.

Escalate cross-tenant exposure, child-safety concerns, credential leakage, payment data or credible legal complaints immediately.
